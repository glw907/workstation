## Coherence question: does anything read as AI-default or template-looking?

Mostly no. The Spectral headline with Karla body, the hanging rail, the plain two-column panel, and the triage rows all read as deliberate, not as a stock SaaS template. The strongest template signal is the tracked small uppercase eyebrow pair (DETAILS / EVIDENCE), a very common generated-UI move, but decision 4 settles it and its execution (0.08em, 50% ink, 4px to content) is restrained. The other mild tell is the unbounded 100+ character body lines inside a wide card, which reads as "default container width" rather than set type. The `max-w-prose` row fixes that.
