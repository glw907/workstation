# Color, surface, and depth ledger

Lens: color, surface, depth. Pages: pr-clean, pr-problem, triage, each in survey and survey-dark, at 1440 and 390.
Method: Playwright (cairn-cms's install) computed every text node's effective color, compositing the ancestor opacity chain over the composited background, then the WCAG ratio. The component styles come from getComputedStyle. Every proposed change was injected and re-rendered in both themes before it went in this table (`audit/color/*-trial.png`). Scripts: `audit/color/measure.cjs`, `comp.cjs`, `trial.cjs`.

Reference ratios. Light: base-content #22262b on base-100 #f7f7f3 is 14.17, on base-200 is 12.96. Dark: 14.67 and 13.25. The theme's own `--color-muted` scores 6.03 and 5.52 in light, 7.59 and 6.86 in dark.

## AI-default and template check

Mostly no. The page avoids gradient heroes, card grids, drop-shadowed tiles, colored icon chips, and decorative accent use. Hierarchy comes from type, ink tiers, and spacing, and the warm grays with a Spectral headline read as a deliberate house palette. The one template-shaped spot: the Decide-first alerts render as the same rounded base-200 panel with a colored left stripe as the headline block, three stacked "card with accent rail" shapes. That is the pattern the soft-tint fix removes. With tinted alerts, the stripe becomes the alert's own DaisyUI language rather than a repeated card motif.
