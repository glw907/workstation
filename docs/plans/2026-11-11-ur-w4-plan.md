## What changes

new files.

the four planner rules with the ten miss tags a planner's miss lines open with (the "class list"

`round.md` for the planner and `round-run.md` for the executor and the conductor.

builder"). The content each file must hold is bounded by the acceptance list and by `D` lines 250
to 259: item 1 and item 2 there for `round.md`, and items 3 to 5 plus the twelve standing answers
for `round-run.md`. The other sources below supply wording and detail, and the executor reads
