   `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS-xxxxxx` form). A `merge` document gives one wait per entry of `heads.json`
   (`repo` is `owner/name`; keep `name` when the owner is `glw907`). The wait runs from
   `meta.json`'s `created`, or from the `at` of the last `returned` record before it, to the `at`
   of the last `approve` or `sendback` record that no later `undo` cancels. A document with no
   standing decision gives no wait. `design` and `decide` documents carry no repo, so count them

   cut as old ones. The store rule gives one wait per PR per round of the document, where the old
   log gave one per decision line: an earlier sendback's wait ends at the `returned` that follows

   on one Notes line, `- Listing full at 300 (older merged PRs missed): <repo>, <repo>`, printed
