Read brief.md in this folder. It is a settled design that the repo's owner approved in
conversation. Write the spec for it to spec.md, by the building-from-specs skill and its guide to
writing a spec.

Nobody can answer a question while you work. Where you would ask, decide, and say in the spec what
you decided. Build nothing and commit nothing: spec.md is the only file you write.

Three files stand in for workstation tools that you can't reach from here:

- .workstation/task-report.txt is what `task-report` prints: the measured minutes of past tasks in
  this repo.
- .workstation/denials.log is the workstation's log of refused commands.
- .workstation/remote.txt describes the repo's remote, example/notes on GitHub.
