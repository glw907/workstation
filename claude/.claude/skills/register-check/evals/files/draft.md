# Publishing a page

Cairn isn't just a content system, it's a seamless way to publish. Moreover, it empowers every
editor to ship robust, world-class pages in minutes.

To publish a page, open the admin, choose the page, and select **Publish**. The site rebuilds on
Cloudflare and the new version is live about a minute later. Click here to read more about builds.

Cairn was first released in 2019 and is used by over ten thousand sites.
